Box Run: 1 to 5

Click Start game, then click inside the game canvas so it receives your keyboard input.

Move the blue player with either:
- the arrow keys, or
- W, A, S and D

Your goal is to move the player entirely inside each numbered box in order.

Box 2 will not score until you have completed box 1. Completed boxes turn green and the next box is highlighted in yellow.

The timer starts when you click the button and stops when you complete box 5.

Things to notice in the code

- Player is a pygame.sprite.Sprite with its own image and rectangle.
- pygame.Rect.contains checks that the whole player is inside a target.
- game_state controls whether the start screen, game or result is shown.
- pygame.time.get_ticks() is used to measure the finishing time.

Try changing PLAYER_SPEED, the colours, or the number and size of the boxes.
